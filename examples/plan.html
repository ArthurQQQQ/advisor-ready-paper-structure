Layout cues for passage retrieval

An English paper plan with Chinese revision notes, based on the example source packet.

Working conclusion

In a paired pilot on 24 questions from single-column PDFs, the reranker with layout cues retrieved the annotated top passage on 15 questions versus 12 for the text-only reranker (E02).

The result describes this pilot. Claims about other layouts, answer accuracy, and the individual contribution of section headings require different evidence.

1. Introduction

Frame the task as selecting an annotated evidence passage from a fixed candidate set. Introduce layout as information for reranking and end with a retrieval question.

G01 待补　等配对评估确定覆盖范围后，再写最后一段的主要发现。

2. Method and evaluation

Follow the input through the shared candidate pool, layout-aware reranker, and top-ranked passage. Define the match criterion before explaining the comparison.

G01 待补　写清问题、候选池和标注的配对条件，以及排除标准。

3. Results

Open with the question that the paired comparison addresses. Describe how to read the table by PDF layout, report the observed differences, and tie them to the joint layout configuration. Present the pilot at its actual scope.

G02 待补　补齐每道题的成对结果和不确定性。按分母解释差异，表格与图例等待核算。
